Seattle Wage Gaps by Race and Gender

This visualization compares average hourly wages for male and female City of Seattle employees across racial groups.

Looking only at overall averages can hide important differences. By comparing wages within racial groups, we can see how large the gender wage gap is for each group. Larger distances between the blue and pink dots indicate larger differences in average pay.

Not all wage gaps are the same size.

Some racial groups show smaller differences between male and female wages, while others show much larger gaps. Comparing groups side-by-side makes these differences easier to see than a single overall average.

Hover over each dot to see the exact average wage and the number of employees represented in that group.

Data Source: Seattle Open Data Portal Wage Data
